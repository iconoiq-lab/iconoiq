What the kits teach

Every form in a temple follows a rule. We make it child's play.

Shilpa Shastra is the classical Indian science of sculpture and temple building: it sets the proportions, poses and symbols of every form. Each ICONOIQ kit explains one idea and lets young hands try it.

  1. 1

    Learn the form

    Picture cards explain what each pose, hand gesture and symbol means.

  2. 2

    Trace and create

    Templates and stencils guide the hand to the right proportions.

  3. 3

    Present and perform

    Build, colour and show it: in class, on stage or at home.
